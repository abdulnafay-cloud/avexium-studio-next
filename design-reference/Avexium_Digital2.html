Process

Four steps, each with a clear outcome, so you always know what happens next.

Weeks 2 to 3

Sketch

  • Three design directions
  • Moodboards and prototypes

Weeks 4 to 8

Build

  • A working preview every week
  • Testing on real devices
Week 9

Launch

  • Launch and monitoring
  • Guidelines and handover
  • Support for the first 30 days
